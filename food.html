TRANSPARENT SERVICE TAX STRUCTURE

How QHP Calculates Delivery Taxes

Our service tax is calculated strictly on the total time taken from when the delivery partner accepts your request to when your order reaches your hands.

STANDARD TIER

Up to 30 Minutes

Fast nearby pickups & quick preparation

₹60 Service Tax

Applied when total elapsed time from order acceptance to doorstep delivery is within 30 minutes.

MEDIUM TIER

31 to 60 Minutes

Moderate distances or busy kitchen hours

₹120 Service Tax

If total pickup and transit time exceeds 30 minutes up to 60 minutes, the tax becomes ₹120.

EXTENDED TIER

Every Extra 30 Mins

Long distance & out-of-city orders

+₹60 per 30 mins

For every additional 30 minutes required, an additional ₹60 service tax is systematically added.

THE COMPLETE FOOD ORDER LIFECYCLE

How Food Delivery Works in QHP

Inside the QHP official app, every food order follows this smooth step-by-step workflow.

1

Select or Name Restaurant

In the app, choose from nearby featured restaurants or manually type any local hotel name, specify menu items, and submit.

2

Acceptance & Call Confirmation

A nearby QHP delivery partner accepts your request and immediately calls you to confirm the order details and hotel location.

3

Pickup & Live GPS Tracking

After confirmation, the partner goes to the restaurant, collects your food, and enables Live GPS Location Tracking on your app screen.

4

Delivery & Time Calculation

Your hot food is delivered to your door! Total active time is calculated, and you pay food costs plus the exact ₹60/30-min tax tier.
