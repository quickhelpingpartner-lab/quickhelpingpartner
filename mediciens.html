TRANSPARENT SERVICE TAX STRUCTURE

How QHP Calculates Medicine Delivery Taxes

Our service tax is calculated strictly on the total time taken from when the delivery partner accepts your request to when your health supplies reach your hands.

STANDARD TIER

Up to 30 Minutes

Fast nearby pharmacy pickups & essential supplies

₹60 Service Tax

Applied when total elapsed time from order confirmation to doorstep delivery is within 30 minutes.

MEDIUM TIER

31 to 60 Minutes

Specialty store sourcing or peak demand hours

₹120 Service Tax

If total pickup and transit time exceeds 30 minutes up to 60 minutes, the service tax becomes ₹120.

EXTENDED TIER

Every Extra 30 Mins

Hard-to-find medicines & out-of-city requests

+₹60 per 30 mins

For every additional 30 minutes required, an additional ₹60 service tax is systematically added.
